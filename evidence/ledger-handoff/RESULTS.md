# Measured hand-off paths: the ledger runs

The Attention Tax, Handoff Tax ledger runs, read by `evidence/ledger-handoff/results.py` (`DESIGN.md` 13.15) into `results.json`. One model, `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, makes every call.

The chain is a surrogate for the hand-off this library decides on, not that hand-off. `single` runs each item's ledger steps in one context, the whole history in every call; the chain runs every five steps in a fresh context that starts from a note the previous context wrote, so a successor takes over the work and no parent reads a result back. The paths are reported as measured: they are not priced, since the registry has no row for this model, and no delegation decision is set beside them, since one model fills every role and no delegate is cheaper per token.

## By depth (300 items, the same keys on both paths)

Tokens are the median over items of each item's summed counts; the chain's note calls count on the chain path. Lost and gained compare the two paths on the same item: lost is an item `single` solves (`final_ok`) and the chain does not.

| depth | items | `final_ok` single | `final_ok` chain | chain lost | chain gained | median prompt tokens, single | median prompt tokens, chain | median completion tokens, single | median completion tokens, chain | median notes | median note tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 100 | 49 | 64 | 18 | 33 | 87,471 | 38,006 | 762.5 | 1,608 | 3 | 163 |
| 50 | 100 | 4 | 18 | 3 | 17 | 517,315 | 101,419 | 2,339 | 4,729 | 9 | 176 |
| 100 | 100 | 0 | 10 | 0 | 10 | 2,029,808 | 206,613 | 10,262 | 10,057 | 19 | 174 |

Reading it:

- The chain solves more items at every depth: 64 against 49 at depth 20, 18 against 4 at 50 and 10 against 0 at 100
- It is not better on every item: it loses 18 of the items the single context solves at depth 20 and gains 33, loses 3 and gains 17 at 50, and loses 0 and gains 10 at 100
- Its median prompt tokens per item are 38,006 against 87,471 at depth 20, 101,419 against 517,315 at 50 and 206,613 against 2,029,808 at 100. Its median completion tokens are higher at depths 20 and 50 (1,608 against 762.5, 4,729 against 2,339), the notes' own calls, and about level at 100 (10,057 against 10,262)

## The hand-off probe (100 items, 12 steps in, 5 steps continued)

Each item continues 5 steps in the same conversation (`history`) or in a fresh one from a note of budget 100, 300 or 900 tokens that the first conversation wrote. All `inc_ok` and all `abs_ok` count the items whose continued steps all hold the flag; lost and gained compare a note arm with `history` on the same item. The prompt median covers the continued steps only; the note's own call, which read the history, is its own column.

| arm | note budget | all `inc_ok` | all `abs_ok` | `inc_ok` lost | `inc_ok` gained | `abs_ok` lost | `abs_ok` gained | note balance kept | median prompt tokens | median note tokens | median note prompt tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `history` | - | 73 | 54 | - | - | - | - | - | 30,827 | - | - |
| `note_100` | 100 | 79 | 58 | 9 | 15 | 9 | 13 | 100 | 7,840.5 | 137 | 5,093 |
| `note_300` | 300 | 77 | 59 | 11 | 15 | 8 | 13 | 99 | 8,077 | 180.5 | 5,093 |
| `note_900` | 900 | 80 | 60 | 7 | 14 | 5 | 11 | 100 | 8,412.5 | 249.5 | 5,093 |

Reading it:

- In aggregate, continuation success is not lower from a note: from a note of budget 100, 300 or 900 tokens, 79, 77 and 80 of the 100 items keep every continued step `inc_ok` and 58, 59 and 60 every step `abs_ok`, against 73 and 54 with the history
- Per item a note loses some of what the history keeps: on all `abs_ok`, `note_100` loses 9 and gains 13, `note_300` loses 8 and gains 13 and `note_900` loses 5 and gains 11; on all `inc_ok` they lose 9, 11 and 7 and gain 15, 15 and 14. So these runs show no aggregate loss from a short note, not a zero loss
- The balance a note states equals the balance after the 12 steps it follows on 100, 99 and 100 of the items
- The continued steps read a median of 7,840.5 to 8,412.5 prompt tokens from a note against 30,827 with the history; in each note arm the note's own call read a median of 5,093 prompt tokens, the 12 steps of history

## Limits

- A synthetic ledger task and one open model in every role, one run per item at temperature 0.7 (the run settings), so the delegate is never cheaper per token than the parent and none is chosen from a pool
- The hand-off is serial: a successor takes over from a note and no parent reads a result back, so these runs measure what a brief carries forward, not the cost or the success of a delegate whose result a parent reads
- No price: the registry (`model_routing/data/models.json`) has no row for `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, so neither flavor's cost is computed here; the token counts per kind let a reader apply any price
